Safety Gear & Protection

Here you can find information about the necessary safety gear for paddle boarding and other water activities.

Essential Safety Gear

When engaging in water activities, it's crucial to have the right safety gear. Here are some essential items you should consider:

  • Life Jacket (Personal Flotation Device)
  • Whistle
  • Waterproof Phone Case
  • Wetsuit or Dry Suit (depending on conditions)

Additional Safety Tips

Here are some additional tips to ensure your safety while enjoying water activities:

  • Always check weather conditions before heading out.
  • Let someone know your plans and expected return time.
  • Stay hydrated and take breaks as needed.
  • Be aware of your surroundings and any potential hazards.
