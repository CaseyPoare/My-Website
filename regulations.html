Rules and Regulations

Here you can find information about the rules and regulations for paddle boarding and other water activities in your area.

General Rules and Regulations

Here are some key rules and regulations to keep in mind:

  • Always wear a properly fitted life jacket.
  • Follow local boating laws and regulations.
  • Respect wildlife and their habitats.
  • Do not operate a watercraft while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Notify authorities immediately in case of an emergency.
  • Follow all posted signs and notices.
  • Do not litter or dispose of waste in the water.
  • Be aware of your surroundings and any potential hazards.

Kitsap & Mason County Regulations

Additionally, here are the specific regulations for Kitsap and Mason Counties:

  • Swimming is only permitted in designated areas.
  • Motorized watercraft must be registered and operate with a valid license.
  • Speed limits apply to all watercraft in designated zones.
