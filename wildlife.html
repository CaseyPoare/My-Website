Wildlife & Nature

Explore the diverse wildlife and natural beauty of Kitsap and Mason Counties. Learn about local species, habitats, and conservation efforts.

Local Wildlife

Kitsap and Mason Counties are home to a variety of wildlife species. Here are some common animals you may encounter:

  • Bald Eagles
  • River Otters
  • Harbor Seals
  • Great Blue Herons
  • Various species of fish and amphibians

  • Wildlife monitoring programs
  • Community education initiatives
